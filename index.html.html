Questions you may have

Where in the world do you work?

We work with real estate businesses worldwide. Everything is done remotely, so your location does not limit us. Just tell us your country and time zone during the discovery call.

Do I have to buy every service?

No. Many clients start with one service, such as ads or a landing page, and add more as results come in. We recommend what fits your business and budget.

Can you work with my existing CRM and tools?

In most cases, yes. Tell us what you use and we will confirm during the discovery call how we can connect it to your marketing and automation.

How do we communicate across time zones?

By email, WhatsApp and scheduled calls. We agree on response times at the start so you always know what to expect.

What happens on the discovery call?

We learn about your business, review your current online presence, and tell you honestly where the biggest gaps are and whether we are a good fit. There is no obligation to continue.

Will you guarantee a number of leads or sales?

No honest agency can promise specific sales. We set realistic targets based on your market and budget, track them every month, and adjust based on the data.
